# Contributing

Suggestions and corrections are welcome.

## Good contributions
- Original tutorials and practical examples.
- Clear, reproducible SEO checklists.
- Useful templates with instructions.
- Corrections to outdated or inaccurate information.
- Relevant, authoritative references that support a specific claim.

## Please do not submit
- Bulk backlink or directory lists.
- Paid-link offers, link exchanges or reciprocal-link schemes.
- Keyword-stuffed anchor text or irrelevant links.
- Scraped articles, copied content or AI-generated filler without review.
- Unsupported ranking guarantees or misleading claims.

## Before submitting
1. Explain the intended reader and practical benefit.
2. Check facts and include sources for external claims.
3. Use descriptive link text and link only where it helps the reader.
4. Confirm you have permission to contribute the material.
5. Disclose commercial relationships or affiliations.

Maintainers may edit or reject contributions that do not meet these standards.
