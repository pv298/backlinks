# Marketing Analytics

## Workflow
1. Clarify the business question and decision the analysis should support.
2. Inventory available sources, date ranges, definitions, currencies, time zones and data gaps.
3. Validate data quality: duplicates, missing values, inconsistent naming, tracking changes and attribution windows.
4. Define a KPI tree connecting business outcomes to funnel metrics.
5. Segment by channel, campaign, audience, creative, geography and time where data supports it.
6. Compare like with like; annotate budget, seasonality, tracking and offer changes.
7. Diagnose movement and distinguish correlation from causation.
8. Present findings with evidence, caveats and recommended actions.
9. Define reporting cadence, owners and alert thresholds.
10. Preserve reproducible calculations and metric definitions.

## Deliverables
Measurement plan, KPI dictionary, dashboard specification, campaign report, insight summary, data-quality notes and action list.

## Guardrails
Never fabricate or silently impute business results. Show formulas and assumptions. Flag missing or inconsistent data. Do not claim causal lift from observational comparisons. Protect personal and confidential customer data.
