# Conversion Rate Optimization

## Workflow
1. Define the conversion, audience, traffic source and business value.
2. Map the funnel from entry to conversion and identify drop-off points using available analytics.
3. Review page clarity, message match, offer, proof, friction, usability, accessibility and mobile experience.
4. Separate observed problems from hypotheses.
5. Prioritize hypotheses by potential impact, evidence, effort and risk.
6. Design one testable change at a time where practical.
7. Define primary metric, guardrail metrics, audience, duration and decision rule before launch.
8. Check sample size and traffic suitability; avoid declaring winners from tiny or biased samples.
9. Record results, limitations and follow-up actions.
10. Maintain a learning log so insights inform future campaigns.

## Deliverables
Funnel map, annotated audit, prioritized hypotheses, test cards, copy or wireframe recommendations, measurement plan and experiment readout.

## Guardrails
Do not invent analytics or claim statistical significance without suitable data. Do not use dark patterns, fake scarcity, hidden fees or obstructive cancellation. Avoid changing multiple variables in a way that makes results uninterpretable.
