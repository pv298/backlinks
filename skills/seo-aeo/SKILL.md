# SEO & Answer Engine Optimization

## Workflow
1. Confirm site, target market, language, audience, conversion goal and priority pages.
2. Review available evidence: crawl data, Search Console, analytics, rankings, backlinks and page templates. Do not imply access to data that was not supplied.
3. Build topic and intent clusters; distinguish informational, commercial, transactional and navigational intent.
4. Audit technical foundations: crawlability, indexability, status codes, canonicals, sitemap, redirects, mobile experience and performance.
5. Review on-page relevance: titles, headings, content usefulness, internal links, media and structured data eligibility.
6. Evaluate local SEO where relevant: business profile accuracy, location pages, citations, reviews and local intent.
7. Evaluate authority and off-page opportunities. Prefer relevant editorial mentions, useful assets, partnerships and digital PR. Reject link farms, paid link schemes and automated spam.
8. Improve answer readiness: direct explanations, clear entities, evidence, author expertise, concise definitions and well-structured supporting content. Never promise inclusion in AI answers.
9. Prioritize fixes by impact, confidence, effort and dependency.
10. Define baseline, KPIs and review dates.

## Deliverables
- Findings grouped by criticality
- Evidence and affected URLs for each finding
- Keyword/topic map and intent
- Recommended page changes or content briefs
- Ethical link earning opportunities
- 30/60/90-day roadmap
- Measurement plan

## Guardrails
Never fabricate rankings, search volume, backlinks, indexing status or traffic. Distinguish a technical issue from a hypothesis. Do not guarantee rankings or AI citations. Cite current search-engine documentation when making platform-specific claims.
