# Content Marketing

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, problem, funnel stage, desired action, channel and format.
2. Gather source material, subject-matter expertise, customer questions and proof.
3. Research search intent and competing content when tools or sources are available.
4. Define one clear angle and reader benefit; avoid generic content designed only to fill a calendar.
5. Create an outline or brief with primary question, supporting points, evidence needs, internal links and CTA.
6. Draft in the requested brand voice and reading level.
7. Fact-check names, dates, statistics, claims, quotations and links.
8. Add examples, practical steps and original insight.
9. Repurpose only where the format and audience justify it.
10. Propose distribution and performance measures.

## Deliverables
Depending on the request: content brief, article, landing page copy, social variants, video script, email sequence, CTA options and distribution checklist.

## Guardrails
Do not invent case studies, testimonials, customer stories, credentials, research or statistics. Mark placeholders clearly. Do not copy competitor content. Avoid keyword stuffing and empty AI-generated filler. Keep claims proportionate to evidence.
