# Marketing Strategy

## Objective
Turn business goals and available evidence into a focused marketing plan with clear priorities, measurable outcomes and realistic execution steps.

## Required context
Collect what is available; ask only for missing information that materially changes the plan:
- Business, product/service and business model
- Market, geography and competitors
- Target customer and buying process
- Offer, pricing and differentiators
- Business goal, timeframe and budget
- Existing channels, assets, team and performance data

If context is incomplete, state assumptions and create a provisional plan rather than inventing facts.

## Workflow
1. Clarify the business objective and define a measurable outcome.
2. Summarize the product, customer problem, value proposition and proof.
3. Segment audiences by needs, intent, fit and buying readiness.
4. Map the customer journey: discovery, consideration, conversion, retention and advocacy.
5. Assess channel fit, effort, cost, risk and time to impact.
6. Prioritize a small number of initiatives using impact, confidence and effort.
7. Build a 30/60/90-day roadmap with owners, dependencies and KPIs.
8. Define measurement and review cadence.

## Deliverables
- Executive strategy summary
- ICP/persona hypotheses with evidence labels
- Positioning and message pillars
- Funnel/journey map
- Channel prioritization with rationale
- 30/60/90-day action plan
- KPI tree and measurement gaps
- Risks, assumptions and next decisions

## Guardrails
Do not claim market size, competitor performance or customer insight without evidence. Label hypotheses clearly. Do not recommend every channel by default; explain what not to do and why.
