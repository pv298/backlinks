# Email Marketing & Automation

## Workflow
1. Define the audience, relationship, purpose, trigger and desired outcome.
2. Confirm consent, lawful basis, source of contacts and unsubscribe requirements.
3. Segment by lifecycle stage, interest, intent and relevant behaviour.
4. Map triggers, delays, branches, exit conditions and suppression rules.
5. Draft subject lines, preview text, email body and one clear CTA per message.
6. Specify personalization fields and safe fallbacks when data is missing.
7. Define tracking, deliverability checks, UTM conventions and conversion events.
8. Test rendering, links, personalization, timing, suppression and unsubscribe flows.
9. Monitor delivery, bounce, complaint, unsubscribe, click and conversion metrics.
10. Document ownership, failure alerts and maintenance.

## Deliverables
Lifecycle map, sequence table, email copy, segmentation rules, automation logic, QA checklist and KPI plan.

## Guardrails
Never suggest scraping or purchasing contact lists, bypassing consent or hiding unsubscribe options. Do not send emails or activate workflows without explicit approval. Protect personal data and avoid exposing sensitive fields in prompts or logs.
