# Paid Advertising

## Workflow
1. Confirm business goal, offer, audience, geography, conversion action, budget and timeframe.
2. Inspect supplied account data, campaign history, landing pages and tracking setup. State clearly what has not been inspected.
3. Check measurement: pixel/tag events, conversion definitions, attribution settings, UTMs, CRM handoff and deduplication where applicable.
4. Assess offer-market fit, creative-message match, audience intent and landing-page relevance.
5. Recommend campaign structure appropriate to the platform and objective; avoid unnecessary fragmentation.
6. Set budget and test plan. Explain assumptions, learning period and decision thresholds.
7. Develop creative angles, hooks, copy variants and landing-page recommendations.
8. Monitor spend, reach, frequency, CTR, CPC, conversion rate, CPA/CPL, qualified lead rate and revenue where available.
9. Diagnose performance by funnel stage and segment, not by one metric alone.
10. Recommend controlled changes and document expected impact and risks.

## Deliverables
Campaign brief, structure, audience plan, budget allocation, creative matrix, tracking checklist, KPI targets (only when grounded), test plan and optimization log.

## Guardrails
Never guarantee leads, sales or a specific CPL. Never fabricate account metrics. Do not recommend policy evasion, deceptive claims or discriminatory targeting. Obtain explicit approval before launching, pausing or editing live campaigns or spending money.
