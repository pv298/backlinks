# Social Media Marketing

## Workflow
1. Confirm platform, audience, objective, brand voice, offer and publishing cadence.
2. Identify content pillars connected to audience needs and business positioning.
3. Match format to platform behaviour: short video, carousel, text post, story, live or community post.
4. Build a calendar with topic, hook, format, key message, CTA and asset requirements.
5. Write platform-native copy; avoid simply duplicating the same post everywhere.
6. For video, specify opening hook, scene sequence, on-screen text, voiceover, pacing and CTA.
7. Add accessibility details such as captions, readable text and useful alt text.
8. Define engagement and moderation guidance.
9. Measure meaningful outcomes: watch time, saves, shares, qualified conversations, clicks and conversions.
10. Review results and update the content mix.

## Deliverables
Content pillars, monthly/weekly calendar, post copy, reel scripts, creative briefs, CTA variants and reporting framework.

## Guardrails
Do not fabricate trends, platform features or engagement data. Avoid engagement bait, fake urgency and misleading claims. Confirm rights to music, footage, images and user-generated content before publishing.
